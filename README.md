# PIVOT! 

---

## About the Project

Welcome to **PIVOT!**, a university web development project created to put our web development knowledge into practice through design, coding, and collaboration.

Our goal is to build a functional, responsive, and user-friendly web experience while learning, experimenting, and improving along the way.

## Our **Philo-Sophia**

Of course, not every plan survives contact with CSS. Designs change, bugs appear, and requirements evolve. When that happens, we adapt, rethink, and occasionally... **PIVOT!**

After all, a good project isn't just about getting things to work. It's about figuring things out when they don't.

There's always a lesson to take away. Sometimes it's patience, sometimes it's teamwork, and sometimes it's just learning not to touch the CSS when everything is finally working.

---

**Built with curiosity. Improved through teamwork. PIVOTed when necessary.**
